Human per-second labels

How to label (from the competition FAQ) - read once
  • One cell = one second (30 frames, 4 of them shown). Label it cheat only when the cheating behaviour dominates that second, otherwise normal (#44.3, #50.3, #63.1). Hover a picture to enlarge it.
  • The first and last movement of an action count as cheat when they already point toward cheating (reaching for the notes or phone, putting them away as part of the cheating) (#63.2, #72.3).
  • Looking under the desk: cheat from the moment the head starts bending down (#82).
  • Using smartwatch: operating / touching it = cheat, even with the eyes on the paper. Only looking at the screen = normal (#72.2).
  • Reading hidden notes: reading them dominates = cheat; mostly writing with a rare glance at the notes = normal (#72.1). An object in the hand is not enough on its own (#63.3).
  • Repeated peeking: repeated glances toward one fixed direction (another participant's paper or notes) = cheat. Looking around in many directions = normal. A single short glance is not automatically cheat (#69, #72.4).
  • Plain writing, thinking, or anything neutral is normal, also inside a cheat video (#36.2, #44.1).
  • Not sure? Mark it unsure (orange): those seconds are left out of training. Nothing is pre-filled: decide every second yourself, no AI suggestions (FAQ #45).
